Measurement level guidance
Nominal: categories without order (e.g. department, gender, region).
Ordinal: ordered categories (e.g. low, medium, high; Likert responses).
Scale: quantitative interval/ratio values where arithmetic differences are meaningful (e.g. income, age, weight).
