## Review method

`config/parked_synthesis_review.json` inventories nine historical operational assertions and 39 distinct synthesis claims. Every claim resolves through its source-surface ID to exact branch, PR, commit, file, and section/row, then through an explicit dependency graph to one or more current evidence nodes.

Evidence nodes distinguish:

- reviewed current episodes from `reviewed_learning_corpus.json`;
- human-confirmed semantic policy;
- candidate-only observations from `candidate_learning_evidence_registry.json`;
- governance and deterministic contracts that do not establish a source outcome.

Review status never changes evidence authority. A claim marked `candidate_supported` remains candidate synthesis. A claim marked `already_integrated_equivalent` defers to the current reviewed episode rather than copying historical wording into Core Knowledge.
