# TikTok Acquisition Breakthrough P57

P57 resumed the blocked P54 TikTok source lab for `Diving lesson` and
`Diving equipment`. It used only unauthenticated public or official surfaces,
carried P54's five historical requests without replay, and recorded every new
outbound attempt in a cumulative sanitized ledger.

## What was proven

Public search-index evidence exposed an official TikTok Creative Center
frontier containing stable video IDs. The official public TikTok oEmbed
endpoint then resolved one such ID to a public creator identity, title and
canonical video identity without a credential, cookie, login or provider
quota. The resolved candidate was generated animal-sports content rather than
a scuba lesson or equipment record, so it was correctly rejected. This proves
only a viable per-URL official verifier; it does not prove topic discovery,
source completion or Minimum Trusted Connection.

Direct channel, creator-profile and Creative Center pages returned redirects
or public application shells without usable multi-record stable identities.
Fifteen predeclared oEmbed candidate attempts and one Creative Center attempt
failed before provider connection in the sandboxed network path. They are
still charged conservatively to P57's outbound-attempt budget.

## Hard stop

P57 used 27 of its 40 authorized new outbound attempts, reached TikTok on 11,
and recorded 16 preconnect failures. Zero records were retained for either
topic. A final ten-record set requires at least ten official verifications in
round one and the same ten in an independent round two, before any additional
discovery. That minimum exceeds the thirteen attempts remaining, so the
Prompt's request-budget stop condition fired.

Phases P57-01 and P57-02 were entered with validated seven-field declarations.
P57-03 through P57-05 were not entered. No TikTok adapter, fixture, source or
run manifest, Registry V3, MTC assessment, Analysis handoff, PR or merge was
created. Registry V1/V2 and all shared runner, validator, manifest, schema and
workflow files remain unchanged. Production storage, approval and scheduling
remain disabled.
