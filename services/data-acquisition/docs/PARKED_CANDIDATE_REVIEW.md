## Why the candidate registry is not lossless

All 11 Candidate Learning Evidence records correctly retain a bounded source outcome, but none is a lossless projection of its parked branch:

- **PR #36 / TikTok Shop:** `CLE-000001` retains the challenge boundary, but not the four candidate surfaces, API authorization boundary, typed counter/price/identity contracts, deterministic normalizer, fixtures, response ledger, or evidence-before-exit CLI.
- **PR #37 / marketplace inventory:** the LINE and NocNoc summaries omit exact sample/request ledgers, unresolved static-versus-rendered extraction, detail-correlation questions, and the Kaidee/Central/cross-border source-role analysis.
- **PR #38 / OTA:** Agoda and Traveloka summaries omit exact query completeness, byte/status ledgers, Booking transfer limits, and discovery-only versus rate-evidence distinctions.
- **PR #39 / coffee:** Roots and Nana summaries omit the parser/fixtures, indexed-field details, request-retention failure ledger, and exact roasted-product/menu/provenance/availability separation.
- **PR #40 / Q-Diving:** three summaries omit the distinct content/service/equipment schemas, parsers, registry changes, relative-date handling, plugin-string diagnostic, transfer hypotheses, and exact record/request ledgers.
- **PR #41 / gap scan:** the Akha summary omits its exact 10-record/request ledger, catalog/detail and variant questions, and the historical cross-domain inventory. The scan's rankings are not preserved as authority.

This is a preservation finding, not a recommendation to merge the parked implementation. Future promotion requires the field-level evidence listed in `config/parked_candidate_review.json` and a separately authorized review.

- Product identity does not imply variant identity; Aquamaster range prices remain ranges.
- Scubadoo service price remains separate from Retail Product & Price, room rates, and demand.

- PR #39's Akha `research-only` status is stale relative to PR #41's later candidate observation.
